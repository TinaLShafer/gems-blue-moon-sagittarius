The Blue Moon in Sagittarius is the second full moon of May. That alone makes it rare. But what makes this one worth sitting with is the company it keeps. Uranus is conjunct the sun in Gemini — the first time in 84 years — and Chiron is at the 29th degree of Aries, the furthest it has traveled. Both are endings. Both are beginnings.

Sagittarius rules belief systems. Not the ones you consciously hold. The ones your body has been organized around for years. The ones that feel less like beliefs and more like the floor beneath you. Just the way things are.

This full moon shines a light on the space between what you say you believe and what your nervous system is actually living. That gap is not a failure. It is information.

🌿 What the sky is actually asking Sagittarius doesn't want the managed version of you. It wants what is true. And with Uranus in Gemini disrupting old mental patterns, and Chiron completing its long passage through Aries, this is a moon that asks: what have you been carrying that was never actually yours to begin with?

You have probably felt something shifting in the last few weeks. A restlessness. A growing inability to maintain certain stories about yourself. A quiet exhaustion with beliefs that once felt like protection but now feel more like weight.

That is not a problem. That is the moon doing its work before it arrives.

🕯️ The Essence

The Blue Moon in Sagittarius is not asking you to build a new belief system tonight. It is asking you to become a little less certain about the ones that have been claiming you. What you find underneath them, when you are ready, is what has always been true.

II
Grounded Earth

Your nervous system shapes what truths you can safely access.

This is the part most approaches to belief work miss entirely. The mind can update the story. The body takes longer. And the body does not update through force.

The inherited belief
Handed down in a moment that felt important enough to organize a life around. Survival. Performance. Fear. Identity protection.
What the mind says
"I am safe." "I deserve rest." "I don't have to earn love." The conscious update. Clear. Chosen. Real.
What the body is still living
Still braced. Still expecting collapse. Still organizing around protection. Not because you are broken. Because the body is doing its job.

When the body is in survival, we don't just feel differently. We perceive differently. A dysregulated nervous system narrows possibility. It filters for danger. It clings to certainty. It interprets ambiguity as threat.

That is why insight alone so rarely creates lasting change. You can consciously decide you believe something different. You can write the new story. But if the body still expects collapse, rejection, or overwhelm, the nervous system will continue organizing around protection.

Not because you are resistant. Because the body learned what to prepare for, and it is still preparing.

🌿 The part that changes everything Once the nervous system softens, people often discover that entirely new beliefs emerge naturally. Not forced. Not rehearsed. Lived. The body does not update through willpower. It updates through safety. That is why my work starts beneath cognition. Not: how do we think differently. But: can the body become safe enough to experience reality differently.

The beliefs that exhaust us the most are almost never entirely our own. They were handed to us. By a parent operating from their own fear. By a teacher who said something careless and certain. By an incident that felt like evidence, so the body wrote it down as law.

By generations of people before you who carried the same thing and passed it forward without knowing they were doing it.

🕯️ The Teaching

The body knows when a belief has outlived its usefulness. There is a particular kind of tiredness that comes with it. Not the tiredness of doing too much. The tiredness of maintaining a story that your system stopped believing a long time ago but your mind keeps insisting on. This moon shines a light on that.

Before You Listen

Sit with these before you close your eyes.

Don't rush to the practice. Let these questions do some quiet work first. You don't need answers. Just a moment of honest looking.

Reflection 01 What belief have you been carrying that exhausts you to maintain? Not the one you chose. The one that feels like it just came with you.
Reflection 02 Where in your body do you feel it? Not as a thought. As a physical sensation. A tightness. A bracing. A place that never quite releases.
Reflection 03 If that belief had an origin — a person, a moment, a season — what comes to mind? You don't have to be certain. Just notice what arises.
Reflection 04 What might be underneath it, if you weren't holding it anymore?

It is asking for the small, honest recognition that something you have been treating as true about yourself was built by someone else, in a moment you may not even remember, during a time when you had no choice but to accept it.

You have a choice now.

This Week's Practice

Following the feeling back to its root.

Anytime this week · when you feel the weight of it
  • Find the belief that exhausts you. You don't need to name it perfectly. Just feel for the weight of it somewhere in your body.
  • Follow the feeling down. Not into the story about it. Into the sensation underneath the thought. Where does it live?
  • Ask quietly, without needing an answer: where did this come from? Let whatever comes, come. A person. A moment. A season. Or just a quality of feeling.
  • Notice whether it feels entirely yours. If something in you says no, or even maybe not entirely, let yourself recognize that.
  • Say to yourself, without force: this does not belong to me. I did not choose this. I am allowed to put it down. Then take one breath and notice what shifts.
🕯️ Seat. Feet. Ground. Begin. 🔔 This practice is not about dramatic release. Sometimes it is just a small loosening. A breath that goes a little deeper than the last one. A sense that the weight is not quite as settled as it was a moment ago. That is enough. That is the work.
